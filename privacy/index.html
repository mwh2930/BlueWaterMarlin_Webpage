BlueWater Marlin is published by Red Oak Media House, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("BlueWater Marlin," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the BlueWater Marlin iOS application, website, support service, and supporting cloud services handle personal information.

Privacy at a glance

  • No account, advertising, cross-app tracking, or in-app behavioral analytics.
  • Your precise GPS fix, saved spots, trip records, outcomes, and notes stay on your device. They are not uploaded to our learning service.
  • Connected features transmit a coarse map area, tile address, station, or chosen forecast coordinate, together with ordinary connection and diagnostic information.
  • Limited operational records are used to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the service—not for advertising, profiling, sale, or model training.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • The App Store privacy label conservatively identifies Coarse Location, Product Interaction, and Other Diagnostic Data as Data Linked to You and used only for App Functionality; none is used for tracking.

1. Scope, controller, and important definitions

This policy covers the BlueWater Marlin iOS application, our website, support communications, and the cloud systems we control for the service. It does not replace the privacy notices of Apple or independent public-data services that receive a request directly.

Red Oak Media House, LLC is the controller of personal information it decides to collect and use. GitHub processes public-website request information as our website hosting provider, and Microsoft processes certain cloud information for us as a service provider. Apple and public-data providers may act independently under their own privacy notices when the app contacts them directly.

In this policy, personal information includes information that identifies, relates to, describes, or can reasonably be linked with a person, device, or household. It may include an IP address, coarse location, request history, support email, or diagnostic record even when it does not contain a name.

2. Information that stays on your device

If you allow location access, the app uses your precise GPS fix on your device to place your boat on the chart and calculate distance and bearing. The app does not automatically upload that GPS fix.

Your Spot Book entries, spot names, Trip Log, catch outcomes, notes, and app preferences are stored locally. We cannot view them through our service. The local copy is removed when you delete it or delete the app. Copies may remain in a personal iCloud or encrypted device backup that you control under Apple's policies.

Information processed only on your device is not collected by Red Oak Media House. If you choose to send a diagnostic report or support attachment, the copy you send is handled as described below.

5. Learning and model data

Customer fishing records are not part of our learning system. The current release configuration disables the customer-learning upload path, provides no sharing switch, and does not use saved spots, trips, outcomes, or notes to train a model. Public ocean observations may be used to develop and evaluate ocean-analysis features without customer records.

If we introduce optional customer-contributed learning in the future, we will update this policy and the applicable App Store privacy information before collection begins, provide a just-in-time explanation in the app, and keep the feature off until the user affirmatively opts in. Withdrawing will be as easy as opting in and will stop future contributions. The feature will be designed around coarse region and structured environmental conditions and outcomes—not exact fishing spots, raw tracks, spot names, free-text notes, identity, or IP addresses as model features.

9. Retention and deletion

The tables above state our ordinary periods. We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, taking account of volume, sensitivity, risk, technical constraints, legal requirements, and dispute needs. We then delete or de-identify it unless law permits or requires continued retention.

  • On-device information remains until you delete the relevant record or the app, subject to backups you control.
  • First-party operational and website diagnostic copies we control are ordinarily kept for no more than 30 days; hosting providers apply their own disclosed retention practices.
  • Support and privacy-request records are ordinarily kept for up to 24 months after closure.
  • A documented security incident, dispute, legal obligation, or legal hold may require a longer period limited to that need.
  • Public data providers and Apple apply their own retention practices to requests they receive directly.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings and continue using features that do not require your position. You can delete saved information in the app or remove the app and its local data. You can manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. You may ask us to delete support correspondence or other personal information we control, subject to security, legal, accounting, and dispute-preservation exceptions.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal information and access it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete information, subject to legal exceptions;
  • receive portable information you provided in an appropriate format;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the basis;
  • opt out of sale, targeted advertising, or qualifying profiling—activities we do not perform;
  • appeal a refusal where applicable; and
  • receive equal service and pricing when exercising privacy rights.

Submit a request by emailing support@bluewatermarlin.com with the subject “Privacy Request.” Describe the right you wish to exercise and the service or communication involved. We will acknowledge and respond within the period required by law—normally within 30 days under GDPR/UK GDPR and 45 days under many U.S. state laws, subject to permitted extensions.

We will request only information reasonably necessary to verify a request. Because the app has no account and operational records are not indexed by name or email, we may be unable to link a particular network record to you. We will explain a denial. Where state law provides an appeal right, reply with “Privacy Appeal” within 60 days of the denial; a different period controls if local law requires it. Authorized agents may submit requests where law permits, subject to verification of authority.

12. Australia

If the Australian Privacy Act 1988 applies, you may request access to or correction of personal information we hold. You may also complain that we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles. Email support@bluewatermarlin.com with the subject “Australian Privacy Complaint” and describe the issue. We will acknowledge the complaint, investigate it fairly, and ordinarily respond within 30 days.

13. EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

BlueWater Marlin is operated from the United States and is currently offered primarily to users in the United States and Australia. If GDPR, UK GDPR, or Swiss data-protection law applies, Sections 6 and 10 describe our purposes, legal bases, and your rights. EEA users can find their authority through the European Data Protection Board. We have not appointed an EEA or UK representative because we do not currently direct regular data-processing activities toward those markets. We will appoint and identify a representative before conducting processing that makes one legally required.

14. Security and incident response

We use measures intended to protect information, including TLS for network connections, data minimization, restricted cloud access, provider IP masking where configured, short operational retention, and review of material service providers. We maintain procedures to investigate suspected incidents and will notify affected people and regulators when law requires. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Children

BlueWater Marlin is a boating and fishing planning tool for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 or intentionally marketed to minors. It has no account, social feed, advertising, or customer-learning upload feature. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us; we will investigate and delete it when required. A parent or guardian may make the request.
