Quick answers

Why is the ocean data dated?

Ocean observations and analyses are processed and quality-checked before publication. Source ages vary, and clouds can delay infrared observations. The app shows source or analysis time where applicable instead of calling older data live.

Will the chart work offshore?

Load your fishing area before leaving the dock. Previously loaded SST, chlorophyll, and sargassum grids can be restored from cache. Currents, wind, tides, live updates, and new areas may require a connection. Cached data remains labelled with source time.

Why does the map show no color?

Check the connection and pass-status chips, try Refresh, then open View app diagnostics from the in-app Help & Support page if the layer still does not load.

How accurate is tap-to-read?

The readout uses the nearest displayed source or analysis grid cell, with no app-side interpolation. Resolution varies by product: MUR SST is about one kilometre, OLCI chlorophyll is about four kilometres where available, and a coarser DINEOF analysis may fill cloud gaps.

Safety

BlueWater Marlin is a fishing planning tool, not a navigation device. Do not use it for navigation, collision avoidance, or safety of life at sea. Satellite observations are delayed and cloud-affected. Always verify against official nautical charts and carry proper safety equipment.
