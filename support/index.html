Quick answers

Why is the satellite pass dated?

Ocean observations are processed and quality-checked before publication. Sea-surface temperature commonly trails real time by one to three days, and clouds can delay it further. BlueWater Marlin shows the source and pass time instead of calling an older pass live.

Will the chart work offshore?

Load your fishing area before leaving the dock. The last successful pass stays cached on your device when service drops and remains marked with its source date. A first load of a new area still requires a data connection.

Why does the map show no color?

Check the connection and pass-status chips, try Refresh, then open View app diagnostics in the in-app Help & Support page if the layer still does not load.

How accurate is tap-to-read?

The readout uses the nearest native satellite cell—about one kilometre for SST and four kilometres for chlorophyll—without inventing an interpolated measurement. Coastal pixels may be blended for display but are not reported as measurements.

How do I manage BlueWater Pro?

Apple handles purchases. Use Restore Purchases on the BlueWater Pro screen, or manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Safety

BlueWater Marlin is a fishing planning tool, not a navigation device. Do not use it for navigation, collision avoidance, or safety of life at sea. Satellite observations are delayed and cloud-affected; tide stations are coastal; waypoints and marks are approximate. Always verify against official nautical charts and carry proper safety equipment.
